How it's calculated

Accepted shares = shares you tender × acceptance ratio, rounded down. This assumes you tender your whole holding. Tax treatment of buybacks differs by period and holder, so check current rules.

How to use the Buyback Calculator

Enter shares held, average buy price, buyback price, acceptance ratio. The calculator updates as you type and shows Buyback proceeds, along with the figures behind it. It runs in your browser, so nothing you enter is sent anywhere. Use the Reset button to go back to the example values.
