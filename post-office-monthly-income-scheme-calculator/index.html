How it's calculated

Monthly income = deposit × rate ÷ 12. The interest is paid out every month and does not compound, and the deposit is returned at the end of the 5-year tenure. The government reviews the rate every quarter, and the default is the rate at the time of writing. The rate stays fixed for the deposit's term.

How the Post Office Monthly Income Scheme works

The Post Office Monthly Income Scheme is a government-backed deposit that pays interest every month for 5 years, then returns your deposit. It suits people who want a steady, safe monthly income, such as retirees.

For example, a deposit of ₹9 lakh at 7.4% pays ₹5,550 a month, or ₹66,600 a year. Over 5 years that adds up to ₹3.33 lakh of interest, and the ₹9 lakh is returned at the end.

Frequently asked questions

Is the monthly income taxable?

Yes. The interest is taxed at your income-tax slab rate.

Can I close the account early?

Yes, after the first year, with a deduction from the deposit that depends on how early you close it.

Can I reinvest the monthly interest?

The scheme itself pays the interest out. Many people set up a recurring deposit to reinvest it automatically and earn interest on it.
