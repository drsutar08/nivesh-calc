How it's calculated

Each monthly deposit earns interest, compounded quarterly, for the months it stays in the account. The maturity value is the sum of all deposits with their interest. Tenure is rounded to whole months.

How to use the RD Calculator

Enter monthly deposit, interest rate (p.a.), tenure. The calculator updates as you type and shows Maturity amount, along with the figures behind it. It runs in your browser, so nothing you enter is sent anywhere. Use the Reset button to go back to the example values.
